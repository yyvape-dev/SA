STYLE BASE v2 (2026-11-07):
Painting technique: painterly digital oil-paint concept art for a game background. Visible thick brushstrokes, rich color, strong light-and-shadow contrast. Not a classical old-master painting, not a photograph, not anime line art.
World: the back town of an empire's capital, a chaotic slum and pleasure quarter like the Kowloon Walled City, built from stacked and patched buildings, iron scaffolding, tangled cables and pipes. Magitech everywhere (magic fused with over-advanced technology): neon magic-circle signs, rune circuits glowing along pipes, lamps that mix crystals and machinery, projected vision screens showing abstract shapes. Colors: dark violet, iron and brass, with neon in violet, green, cyan and pink, and a few warm windows. Not medieval, not a modern city, not a Japanese Showa-era alley.
View: a flat front elevation facing the viewer straight on. No perspective, no vanishing point, no street receding into the distance. Vertical lines stay vertical, horizontal lines stay horizontal.
Lighting (most important): 60 to 70 percent of the image is in deep shadow. At most 2 or 3 strongly glowing signs or lamps; everything else is dim and low in contrast. Dense detail is grouped and sits in the shadows.
Signs and screens show only abstract symbols and shapes, never readable letters. Do not draw any people, UI or text.
